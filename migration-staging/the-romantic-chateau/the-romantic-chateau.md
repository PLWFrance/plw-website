## Dance under the stars!

Romantic is a perfect venue for small weddings in France. With high spec accommodation, private facilities, courtyard, barn and even its own church!  So, Romantic ticks all the boxes. Set in the glorious Lot countryside this gem houses up to 25 and features an exterior dancing area for those hoping to spend the night dancing under the stars.

Mid-week two-night weddings at Romantic, include (for Perfect Little Wedding events) lots for free! Such as: breakfast on your wedding day for you and your on-site guests; outdoor dance floor and outdoor lighting; a keg of beer; and the outdoor music system.  What more could you ask for from a venue for small weddings in France?  All you have to do is get the party started!
